Interactive simulations

Learning physics by
moving things around.

A growing set of browser-based simulations for understanding the physical world. Adjust the inputs, drag the elements, watch the numbers respond. Free to use, nothing to install.

Available now
01

Vector Bench

Build vectors from magnitude and direction, then add them head to tail or by the parallelogram method. Resolve any vector into its x and y components and watch the resultant update as you drag.

02

Motion Lab

A rigid-body sandbox. Place circles, triangles and rectangles, then launch one of them under adjustable gravity, friction and air resistance. Displacement, velocity and acceleration plot in real time as it bounces off the obstacles you built.
